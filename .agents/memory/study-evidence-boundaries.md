---
name: Study evidence boundaries
description: How to interpret conflicting thesis sources and historical XUL10 conventions without implying account-risk validation.
---

Keep table-specific provenance when using the thesis. Do not silently reconcile conflicting main-table and appendix values, formulas, or treatment of margin-call failures by choosing the larger value or a more convenient definition.

**Why:** The evidence examination found substantive differences between the main productivity table and image-only calculation appendices, not merely rounding. Matching a constant to one table does not replicate the study or establish a calibrated risk model.

**How to apply:** Use `docs/mrg-study-evidence.html` for the evidence map and dual printed/PDF page references. A new model proposal must identify its authoritative dataset, metric denominators, costs, and failure handling before assigning weights; implementation needs separate approval.

Historical point/contract conventions and current feed/broker verification are separate claims.

**Why:** The supplied thesis can establish its historical price-unit convention, while a live quote response does not establish contract size, tick value, settlement rate, or account margin rules.

**How to apply:** Attribute units and contract examples to their historical source. Do not treat quote precision, a symbol alias, or daily gold-reference ATR as permission to compute account outcomes or assign study scores to custom targets/grid spacing.
