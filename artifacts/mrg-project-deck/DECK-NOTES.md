# MRG project presentation

## Communication
- Eight Indonesian slides for live project demonstrations and internal explanation.
- Outcome: understand what MRG does, what its data means, and the boundaries of its current readiness.
- Content slides use assertion titles; the cover and closing name the project and next phase.
- No presenter notes requested. Manifest speakerNotes remain empty.

## Visual direction
Editorial product presentation derived from the existing Newsmaker MRG app.
Light content slides, navy cover/closing, one main idea per slide.

- Background #f7f8fb, ink #17345a, brand blue #1061b3, navy #083d79.
- Surface #eaf2fb and white #ffffff also appear in the source UI.
- Newsreader display, DM Sans body: the active families in artifacts/mrg/src/index.css.
- Type roles: cover 64pt, content title 40pt, closing 48pt, body 20–22pt, caption 16pt.
- Grid: 120-unit margins, repeated content header at x120/y160.
- Motifs: blue/navy framing panels and numbered editorial section labels.
- No fabricated financial charts, decorative market statistics, stock art, or new logos.
